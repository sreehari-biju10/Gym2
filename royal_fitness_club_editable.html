About
the Trainer

Coach Abesh

With over a decade of hands-on experience in strength training, functional fitness, and body transformation, Coach Abesh is the driving force behind Royal Fitness Club Gym. A certified personal trainer and nutrition consultant, Abesh has helped hundreds of clients across Puthupally and beyond redefine their physical limits and build the discipline to sustain a healthy lifestyle.

His philosophy is simple: real results come from consistent effort, smart programming, and the right mindset. Whether you are a complete beginner or a seasoned athlete, Abesh crafts personalised training plans tailored to your body type, goals, and schedule — always pushing you to become a better version of yourself.
